Still Life

What would dead artists do? Ask Van Gogh, Frida, Picasso, Monet and friends anything about art or life, and they answer from the afterlife in a group chat. Every joke is built on a real fact from their lives. Every word is made up.

I made it as a free giveaway: something people want to pass around, that leads back to my studio and Spice Art Club without feeling like an ad. It went through six looks before it felt right, and most of the work was taking things away.

  • The castArtists drift online and away, but never the one you’re talking to. Name someone and they show up.
  • The voiceStoryteller comedy rooted in real lives. Ask the same thing twice and they call you on it.
  • ReadingTap an answer and everything else folds away in one smooth, slightly bouncy move.
  • LessOne + button holds your question, ready-made ones and Surprise me. Tone on tone, so the answers do the talking.
My roleConcept, voice and humor direction, interaction design, motion and visuals. Designed and built with Claude.

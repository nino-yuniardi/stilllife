Still Life

A group chat with the greats who aren’t around anymore. In the artists room, Van Gogh, Frida, Picasso and Monet answer questions about art and life. In the designers room, Paul Rand, Saul Bass, Massimo Vignelli and Coco Chanel take on the client who wants the logo bigger. Every joke is built on a real fact from their lives. Every word is made up.

I made it as a free giveaway: something people want to pass around, that leads back to my work without feeling like an ad. It went through seven looks before it felt right. The last change made it work like a real chat app, and most of the work was taking things away.

  • The castOpen straight into the group chat. Tap back to see everyone, then message one privately. Name someone and they show up.
  • The voiceStoryteller comedy rooted in real lives. Ask the same thing twice and they call you on it.
  • ReadingReplies arrive one at a time, and the chat follows along from each answer’s first line.
  • LessOne typeface for every reply. Each person gets a colour, and the group icon is made of whoever is online.
My roleConcept, voice and humor direction, interaction design, motion and visuals. Designed and built with Claude.
